Federal Länderöffnungsklausel (§ 249 Abs. 3 BauGB)

Government bill; Bundestag roll call 465 : 114 : 3 on 27.06.2014; deadline for Landesgesetze 31.12.2015.

Period
08.04.2014 – 01.08.2014
Status
in force 01.08.2014

Government bill; Bundestag roll call 465 : 114 : 3 on 27.06.2014; deadline for Landesgesetze 31.12.2015.

Steps

  1. 08.04.2014
    The federal cabinet adopts the bill. The Bavarian cabinet adopts its own draft the same day.
    BT Drs 18/1590
  2. 11.04.2014
    BR Government bill, "besonders eilbedürftig"
    BR Drs 155/14
  3. 05.05.2014
    BT Gesetzentwurf
    BT Drs 18/1310
  4. 08.05.2014
    BT 1st reading without debate (batch referral, TOP 24). Lead committee: Umwelt, Naturschutz, Bau und Reaktorsicherheit; also Ernährung und Landwirtschaft, and Wirtschaft und Energie
    BT Plenarprotokoll 18/33
  5. 21.05.2014
    BT Public hearing (below)
  6. 23.05.2014
    The Bundesrat recommends rejecting the federal bill.
    BR Drs 155/14(B)
  7. 23.05.2014
    Morlok defends the federal clause in the Bundesrat, which still recommends rejecting it.
    BR Plenarprotokoll 922
  8. 28.05.2014
    BT Bundesrat statement and government reply
    BT Drs 18/1580
  9. 06.06.2014
    BT Later referral under § 80 GO-BT
    BT Drs 18/1702
  10. 25.06.2014
    BT Committee recommends adoption. For: CDU/CSU, SPD. Against: LINKE, GRÜNE and one CDU/CSU member. Rapporteurs: Kai Wegner (CDU/CSU), Matthias Miersch (SPD), Ralph Lenkert (LINKE), Peter Meiwald (GRÜNE)
    BT Drs 18/1900
  11. 27.06.2014
    The Bundestag passes the bill, 465 : 114 : 3. The LINKE Entschließungsantrag 18/1903 is rejected by roll call, 108 : 467 (final result; announced in the chamber as 111 : 464).
    BT Plenarprotokoll 18/44 BT Drs 18/1903
  12. 03.07.2014
    BR Bundestag's decision sent to the Bundesrat
    BR Drs 294/14
  13. 11.07.2014
    The Bundesrat does not call the Vermittlungsausschuss.
    BR Drs 294/14(B)
  14. 15.07.2014
    Ausfertigung. Promulgated 18.07.2014, in force 01.08.2014

Facts and links

led to1

  • Bavarian 10H law (Art. 82 BayBO; Drs 17/2137) interpretation

    Bayern was the only Land that used § 249 Abs. 3 BauGB before the deadline of 31.12.2015.

    BT Ausarbeitung der Wissenschaftlichen Dienste WD 7 - 3000 - 042/19, p. 3

resulted from1

  • The federal coalition agreement CDU/CSU-SPD promises the Länderöffnungsklausel. interpretation

    The clause came through the CDU/CSU–SPD coalition agreement, not through BR-Drs 569/13, which was never voted on. The government's answers cite the agreement.

    BT Drs 18/268, p. 2
