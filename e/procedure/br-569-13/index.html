Opening clause for height-based distances; factor 10 appears only in the Begründung.

Steps

  1. 02.07.2013
    Joint cabinet meeting of Bayern and Sachsen in Munich. Joint Bundesrat bill, with a cover letter from Seehofer.
    BR Drs 569/13
  2. 05.07.2013
    Bundesrat, 912th session, TOP 88: no debate, referred to committees.
    BR Plenarprotokoll 912
  3. 27.04.2018
    The Bundesrat declares BR-Drs 569/13 erledigt.
    BR Plenarprotokoll 967 BR Drs 569/13
