Steps

  1. 27.05.2014
    Gesetzentwurf der Staatsregierung.
    BY Drs 17/2137
  2. 04.06.2014
    1st reading (PlPr 17/19, pp. 1213–1225), introduced by Innen- und Bauminister Joachim Herrmann. Speakers: Christine Kamm and Martin Stümpfig (GRÜNE), Otmar Bernhard, Erwin Huber and Otto Lederer (CSU), Natascha Kohnen (SPD), Eva Gottstein (FW). A joint Grüne/FW/SPD motion to take it off the agenda failed.
    BY Plenarprotokoll 17/19
  3. 03.07.2014
    Hearing in the Wirtschaftsausschuss (chair Erwin Huber): 12 experts, 11 against.
  4. 10.07.2014–06.11.2014
    Committees for Wirtschaft (lead), Kommunales/Inneres, Umwelt and Verfassung: each voted CSU yes, SPD/FW/GRÜNE no. CSU amendments 17/3415, 17/3416, 17/3417 (Kreuzer, Huber, Freller) incorporated. Berichterstatter Otmar Bernhard (CSU), Mitberichterstatter Martin Stümpfig (GRÜNE).
    BY Drs 17/4099 BY Drs 17/3415 BY Drs 17/3416 BY Drs 17/3417
  5. 12.11.2014
    The Bayerischer Landtag passes 10H, 92 : 63.
    BY Plenarprotokoll 17/29 BY Drs 17/4198 BY Drs 17/4199
  6. 17.11.2014
    Law signed by Seehofer. In force 21.11.2014.
