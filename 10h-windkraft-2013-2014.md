# 10H rule and the Länderöffnungsklausel (2013–2016)

Research of 2026-10-11. Sources: Bundestag DIP API (queried directly, public key), PDFs of the
Drucksachen and Plenarprotokolle, the Bayerischer Landtag, BayVerfGH, Sachsen's media service, and the
Analysen of the Wissenschaftliche Dienste. The `ka` corpus was not used: the stick was not mounted,
and the corpus holds no Bayern/Sachsen/Bund records from 2013–2016 yet.

Markers: **[secondary]** = secondary source only, **[not verified]** = not confirmed.

## Checking the starting thesis

| Claim | Finding |
|---|---|
| Sachsen and Bayern proposed 10H for the first time in July 2013 | **Mostly right, with nuances.** Seehofer had already ordered a review of height-based distances on 05.03.2013, after meeting the BI alliance "Gegenwind Unterfranken" (BY LT-Drs 16/16465). Sachsen had tried alone in the Bundesrat in March 2013 (BR-Drs 206/13: abolish the privilege altogether), and the Bundesrat refused to introduce it. The joint Bundesrat bill of 02.07.2013 (BR-Drs 569/13) **does not set 10H**. It is an opening clause for "angemessene höhenbezogene" distances. Only the Begründung names **factor 10** as the example and as the "Obergrenze der Angemessenheit" (example: 150 m height means 1,500 m). |
| 10 × total height, 200 m means 2 km | Right. Bavaria defines H as hub height plus rotor radius (Art. 82 Abs. 2 BayBO). |
| The push led the Bund to add a clause to the BauGB in 2014 | **Right as to the result, the route was different.** BR-Drs 569/13 was never voted on and was declared erledigt in 2018. The clause came through the **Koalitionsvertrag CDU/CSU-SPD** (27.11.2013) at the CSU's insistence. **The Bundesrat recommended rejecting the federal bill** (23.05.2014), but did not block it (it was an Einspruchsgesetz). Deadline for the Länder: Landesgesetze promulgated **by 31.12.2015**. |
| Bayern put 10H into law in November 2014 | Right. Landtag vote 12.11.2014, law of 17.11.2014, **in force 21.11.2014**. Bayern was the **only** Land that used the clause. |

## Federal level: the law

**Gesetz zur Einführung einer Länderöffnungsklausel zur Vorgabe von Mindestabständen zwischen
Windenergieanlagen und zulässigen Nutzungen**, DIP Vorgang 59400, initiative: Bundesregierung, Einspruchsgesetz.
