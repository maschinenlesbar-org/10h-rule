## Role in the 10H story

- Saxon interior minister: he signed the answer to Kleine Anfrage [6/2391](https://kleineanfragen.de/sachsen/6/2391-neubau-von-windkraftanlagen-am-stausee-radeburg-und-dem-naherholungsgebiet-brettmuehlenteich) (07.09.2015) as Staatsminister des Innern (kleineanfragen.de text copy) ([Sachsen](../sachsen.md)). The letterhead reads „Der Staatsminister"; the OCR of the signature is garbled. Other SMI answers show the same letterhead and his signature cleanly, e.g. Drs 6/196 (published 05.11.2014) and [Drs 6/3673](https://kleineanfragen.de/sachsen/6/3673-landesamt-fuer-verfassungsschutz-sachsen-personalausstattung) (January 2016), also checked in the kleineanfragen.de text copies only. No Bundesrat protocol of 2013–2016 (sittings 905–960) names him. The 1,000 m decree of 12.07.2013 was a joint decree of the economics and interior ministries ([Sachsen PM](https://www.medienservice.sachsen.de/medien/news/185902)) ([Sachsen](../sachsen.md)).
