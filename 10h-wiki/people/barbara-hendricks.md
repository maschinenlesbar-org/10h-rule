## Role in the 10H story

- Federal minister for the environment and building (BMUB). The federal government's answer in [BT-Drs 18/268](https://dserver.bundestag.de/btd/18/002/1800268.pdf) (10.01.2014), p. 28, lists "BMUB / BM'in / Dr. Barbara Hendricks"; she spoke as „Dr. Barbara Hendricks, Bundesministerin für Umwelt, Naturschutz, Bau und Reaktorsicherheit" on 24.06.2014 ([PlPr 18/41](https://dserver.bundestag.de/btp/18/18041.pdf), p. 3604 D).
- Her ministry led the bill: „Federführend ist das Bundesministerium für Umwelt, Naturschutz, Bau und Reaktorsicherheit." (cover letter of the Chancellor, [BT-Drs 18/1310](https://dserver.bundestag.de/btd/18/013/1801310.pdf)).
- No act of her own on the clause found ([Open questions](../open-questions.md)). She did not speak in the Bundestag debate of 27.06.2014 ([PlPr 18/44](https://dserver.bundestag.de/btp/18/18044.pdf), pp. 3931–3945). In the Bundesrat the BMUB was represented by Parl. Staatssekretär Florian Pronold on 23.05.2014 ([BR-PlPr 922](https://dserver.bundestag.de/brp/922.pdf), attendance list) and 11.07.2014 ([BR-PlPr 924](https://dserver.bundestag.de/brp/924.pdf)); neither protocol records a speech by her. The government's reply to the Bundesrat ([BT-Drs 18/1580](https://dserver.bundestag.de/btd/18/015/1801580.pdf)) carries no minister's name. Her Bundestag speeches of 31.01., 02.04., 10.04. and 24.06.2014 (PlPr 18/12, 18/25, 18/30, 18/41) do not mention wind distances or the clause.
