## Role in the 10H story

- Federal economics minister (BMWi). First speaker in the joint debate of 27.06.2014 on the EEG reform and the clause (TOP V a–d) ([PlPr 18/44](https://dserver.bundestag.de/btp/18/18044.pdf), p. 3932 A). His speech is about the EEG and does not mention the clause ([Federal clause 2014](../bund-2014.md)).
- Erklärung nach § 30 GO after the debate (p. 3944 C – 3945 A): a reply to Oliver Krischer on the BAFA staff for the Besondere Ausgleichsregelung, not on the clause.
