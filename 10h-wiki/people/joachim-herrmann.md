## Role in the 10H story

- Innen- und Bauminister. Introduced the government's 10H bill [BY Drs 17/2137](https://www.bayern.landtag.de/www/ElanTextAblage_WP17/Drucksachen/Basisdrucksachen/0000001000/0000001464.pdf) in the 1st reading of the 10H bill on 04.06.2014 (PlPr 17/19, pp. 1213–1225) ([Bayern: Art. 82 BayBO](../bayern-10h.md)).
- 11.07.2014: Bundesrat speaker for Bayern in the 2nd passage of the federal law ([BR-PlPr 924](https://dserver.bundestag.de/brp/924.pdf), [BR-Drs 294/14(B)](https://dserver.bundestag.de/brd/2014/0294-14B.pdf)) ([Federal clause 2014](../bund-2014.md)).
