## Role in the 10H story

- Parliamentary state secretary, BMU. She gave the federal government's answers to four of the five Schriftliche Fragen on wind distances in WP 17 ([Bundestag questions](../anfragen-bundestag.md)). Each is checked against the answer heading in the PDF:
  - SF 94, Kirsten Tackmann (LINKE), answered 04.10.2012: no uniform 1,000 m, because "die bestehenden gesetzlichen Rahmenbedingungen [gewährleisten] einen ausreichenden Schutzabstand" ([BT-Drs 17/10925](https://dserver.bundestag.de/btd/17/109/1710925.pdf)).
  - SF 129, Veronika Bellmann (CDU), answered 05.10.2012: flat distances "nicht zielführend" ([BT-Drs 17/10968](https://dserver.bundestag.de/btd/17/109/1710968.pdf)).
  - SF 142, Kirsten Tackmann (LINKE), answered 09.10.2012: practice in the Länder ([BT-Drs 17/10968](https://dserver.bundestag.de/btd/17/109/1710968.pdf)).
  - SF 82, Thomas Gambke (GRÜNE), answered 07.10.2013: Bavarian turbines closer than 10× hub height; no information ([BT-Drs 17/14821](https://dserver.bundestag.de/btd/17/148/1714821.pdf)).
