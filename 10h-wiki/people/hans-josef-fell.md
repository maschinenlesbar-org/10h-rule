## Role in the 10H story

- Former Grüne MdB. Press reports name him, with Patrick Friedl, as bringing the Popularklage Vf. 14-VII-14 against the 10H law (received 20.11.2014) **[secondary]**. The court upheld 10H on 09.05.2016 ([Vf. 14-VII-14](https://www.bayern.verfassungsgerichtshof.de/media/images/bayverfgh/14-vii-14-entscheidung-u.pdf)) ([Bayern: Art. 82 BayBO](../bayern-10h.md), [Open questions](../open-questions.md)).
