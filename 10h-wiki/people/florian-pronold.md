## Role in the 10H story

- Parliamentary state secretary, BMUB. He answered every Schriftliche Frage on wind distances in 2014, the year of the federal clause ([Bundestag questions](../anfragen-bundestag.md)). Each is checked against the answer heading in the PDF:
  - SF 55/56, Egon Jüttner (CDU), answered 08.01.2014: infrasound; cites the coalition agreement ([BT-Drs 18/268](https://dserver.bundestag.de/btd/18/002/1800268.pdf)).
  - SF 38, Veronika Bellmann (CDU), answered 24.02.2014: the Bavarian 10H cabinet decision is a "declaration of intent"; the Meseberg timetable ([BT-Drs 18/680](https://dserver.bundestag.de/btd/18/006/1800680.pdf)).
  - SF 47, Egon Jüttner (CDU), answered 27.05.2014: 2,000 m "as the WHO demands" ([BT-Drs 18/1590](https://dserver.bundestag.de/btd/18/015/1801590.pdf)).
  - SF 72–74, Christian Kühn (GRÜNE), answered 30.05.2014, one answer to all three: BBSR calculation for 2,000 m in Bayern; no other Land known to plan using the clause ([BT-Drs 18/1684](https://dserver.bundestag.de/btd/18/016/1801684.pdf)).
