Sachsen adopted a 1,000 m rule only in 2022, under the 2020 version of the clause.

## Saxon Landtag questions

- **WP 6 (2014–2016):** wind questions exist, mostly from AfD, Linke and Grüne. None has 10H or § 249 Abs. 3 BauGB in
  its title, but two pass on local calls to use the clause: 6/2391 (Beger, AfD) quotes a Kreistag Meißen resolution of
  18.09.2014, and 6/2491 (Urban, AfD) asks about a Vogtlandkreis Kreistag resolution; see the table below. The others
  found do not mention 10H or § 249 Abs. 3 in their text copies: 6/1748 "Windenergieanlagen in Sachsen" (Urban, AfD),
  6/1905 and 6/2381 "Windpotentialstudie" (Wild, AfD), 6/2837 "Windkraftanlagen in Waldgebieten" (Urban, AfD), and
  6/4052, 6/4062, 6/4063 (Böhme, LINKE).
- **WP 5 (2012–2014):** eight Kleine Anfragen on siting and distances; see the table below. Pinka's two questions of
  summer 2014 are the closest to 10H. On 5/14687 the SMI said the land left under 10H could not be stated while the
  regional plans were at an early stage. On the follow-up 5/14898, about whether the government had commissioned a
  study, it refused to answer at all: the question concerned "mögliche Gesetzgebungsvorhaben", on which "die
  Willensbildung der Staatsregierung noch nicht abgeschlossen ist" (Art. 51 Abs. 2 SächsVerf, "Kernbereich exekutiver
  Eigenverantwortung").
