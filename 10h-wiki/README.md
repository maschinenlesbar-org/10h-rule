# 10H Wiki

How Bayern got a wind turbine distance of ten times the turbine height. The path runs from Seehofer's review order in
March 2013, through a Bundesrat bill and the federal coalition agreement, to § 249 Abs. 3 BauGB (2014) and Art. 82
BayBO (2014).

**10H** means that a wind turbine loses its privileged status in the outer zone (§ 35 Abs. 1 Nr. 5 BauGB) if it stands
closer to housing than ten times its total height. Bayern defines the height as hub height plus rotor radius. A 200 m
turbine therefore needs 2,000 m.

## Checking the starting thesis

| Claim | Verdict | Finding |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sachsen and Bayern proposed 10H for the first time in July 2013 | partly | Seehofer had already ordered a review of height-based distances on 05.03.2013 ([Origin](origin-bayern-2013.md)). Sachsen had tried alone in March 2013 and failed. The joint Bundesrat bill of 02.07.2013 (BR-Drs 569/13) **does not set 10H**. It is an opening clause for "angemessene höhenbezogene" distances. Factor 10 appears only in its Begründung, as an example and as the "Obergrenze der Angemessenheit". |
| 10 × total height; a 200 m turbine needs 2 km | correct | Bayern defines H as hub height plus rotor radius (Art. 82 Abs. 2 BayBO). |
| The push led the Bund to add a clause to the BauGB in 2014 | result yes, route no | BR-Drs 569/13 was never voted on and was declared erledigt in 2018. The clause came through the CDU/CSU-SPD **coalition agreement** of 27.11.2013. The **Bundesrat recommended rejecting** the federal bill but did not block it. Deadline for the Länder: Landesgesetze promulgated by **31.12.2015**. |
| Bayern put 10H into law in November 2014 | correct | Landtag vote 12.11.2014, law of 17.11.2014, in force 21.11.2014. Bayern was the only Land that used the clause. |

## How this was researched

- **Bundestag and Bundesrat:** the DIP API was queried directly (title and descriptor searches, WP 17–18), and every
  document was read in its PDF.
- **Landtage and research services:** web research. Key links were checked.
- **FragDenStaat:** searched through its public API.
- **`ka` corpus:** not used. It holds no Bund, Bayern or Sachsen records from 2013–2016 yet.
- **Full report:** the combined report this wiki is based on is
  [../10h-windkraft-2013-2014.md](../10h-windkraft-2013-2014.md).
