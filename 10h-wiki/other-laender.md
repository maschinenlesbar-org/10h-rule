# Other Länder

Bayern was the only Land that used the clause before the deadline of 31.12.2015. The federal government knew of no
other Land planning to use it as early as May 2014
([BT-Drs 18/1684](https://dserver.bundestag.de/btd/18/016/1801684.pdf)). In 2019 the Bundestag research service
confirmed that Bayern remained the only one
([WD 7-042/19](https://www.bundestag.de/resource/blob/644560/b58b8d097cbff1d160fe4f56e825efff/wd-7-042-19-pdf-data.pdf)).

| Land | What happened | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sachsen | Announced 10H, then dropped it after the 2014 election. See [Sachsen](sachsen.md). | verified |
| Hessen | FDP Gesetzentwurf Drs 19/2070 (16.06.2015) for 10H in the Hessian building code. Hearing 03.12.2015, with a statement by the Stiftung Umweltenergierecht. Not adopted. | **[outcome not verified]** |
| Niedersachsen | FDP Antrag [Drs 17/1973](https://www.landtag-niedersachsen.de/Drucksachen/Drucksachen_17_2500/1501-2000/17-1973.pdf) (16.09.2014), "Eigentum schützen – Anwohnerinteressen berücksichtigen …", calling for 10H. | verified |
| Brandenburg | The government refused to use the clause. BVB/Freie Wähler motions: 29.04.2015 ("Gesetzliche Regelung für Mindestabstände … endlich umsetzen") and 20.11.2015 (Bundesrat initiative to extend the deadline). Volksinitiative "Rettet Brandenburg" (10H, no turbines in forests). In 2016 the Landtag research service found no remaining route through a Land statute; see [Research services](research-services.md). | **[partly secondary]** |
| Mecklenburg-Vorpommern | Kleine Anfrage of Johann-Georg Jaeger (GRÜNE), "Abstandsregelungen für Windkraftanlagen", [Drs 6/4411](https://www.dokumentation.landtag-mv.de/Parldok/dokument/36550/abstandsregelungen-f%C3%BCr-windkraftanlagen.pdf) (07.09.2015; answered by the Minister für Energie, Infrastruktur und Landesentwicklung by letter of 03.09.2015). Its preamble says a Volksinitiative demands 10H and a parallel Volksbegehren 2,000 m. The government answered that 2,000 m would leave "kaum noch Raum für moderne Windenergieanlagen" (wind areas of 600, 0, 100 and 0 ha in the four planning regions) and that 10H would amount to "einer pauschalen Höhenbegrenzung"; Mecklenburg-Vorpommern already plans with 1,000 m. The Volksinitiative's own Drucksache (reported as Drs 6/4450) was not checked. **[secondary]** | Drs 6/4411 verified; Volksinitiative **[secondary]** |
| Schleswig-Holstein | Later Volksinitiative for larger distances (10H, at least 1,000 m). The Landtag research service judged it admissible in 2018; see [Research services](research-services.md). | verified |
| Nordrhein-Westfalen | No use or debate found. | **[not verified]** |
