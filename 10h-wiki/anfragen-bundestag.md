# Bundestag questions

No Bundestag Kleine Anfrage deals with 10H alone. The topic ran through Schriftliche Fragen (SF) and two broader
Kleine Anfragen (KA), one from the Grüne and one from the Linke.

Found through the DIP API (title searches for Windkraft, Windenergie, Mindestabstand, Abstand, Länderöffnungsklausel,
10H and others, plus the descriptor "Windenergieanlage", WP 17–18). Each answer was read in the PDF.

"Answered" is the date in the answer's own heading ("Antwort … vom …"). For the Kleine Anfragen it is the date of the
ministry's cover letter. The Drucksache appeared a few days later. "Answered by" is taken from the printed answer
heading in each PDF (checked 2026-10-12); DIP gives the same names.

## Patterns

- **The 2012–2013 government line:** no flat distances; TA Lärm and case-by-case planning are enough. This was before
  the coalition agreement.
- **After November 2013:** the answers cite the coalition agreement and treat the clause as settled.
- **Two state secretaries answered almost every question on distances.** Katherina Reiche (BMU) answered four of the
  five Schriftliche Fragen in WP 17. Ursula Heinen-Esser answered the fifth, a noise question. In WP 18 the answers
  come from the newly formed BMUB, whose remit now included building, and Florian Pronold answered all four questions
  of 2014. The one question from 2016 asks for approval figures and went to the BMWi (Uwe Beckmeyer). Both Kleine
  Anfragen were sent by the BMWi on behalf of the government.
- **The askers come in two groups.** CDU members (Bellmann, Jüttner) push for larger distances. Grüne and Linke
  members (Gambke, Kühn, Tackmann, Lay, Bulling-Schröter) ask about the effects on wind expansion.
