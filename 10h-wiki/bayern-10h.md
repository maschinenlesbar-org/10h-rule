# Bayern: the 10H law (Art. 82 BayBO)

**Gesetz zur Änderung der Bayerischen Bauordnung und des Gesetzes über die behördliche Organisation des Bauwesens, des
Wohnungswesens und der Wasserwirtschaft.**

- Law of 17.11.2014, published in GVBl 2014, 478, in force 21.11.2014.
- [Beratungsverlauf on the Landtag site](https://www.bayern.landtag.de/webangebot3/views/vorgangsanzeige/vorgangsanzeige.xhtml?gegenstandid=109428)

## Content

- **Art. 82 Abs. 1–2:** the privilege under § 35 Abs. 1 Nr. 5 BauGB applies only if the turbine keeps at least 10 H
  from residential buildings in Bebauungsplan areas, inside built-up areas, and in certain outer-zone settlements.
  H is hub height plus rotor radius.
- **Art. 82 Abs. 3–4:** existing land-use plans with wind concentration zones; municipalities can go below 10H through
  a Bebauungsplan.
- **Art. 82 Abs. 5:** duty to seek agreement with neighbouring municipalities. Declared void in 2016.
- **Art. 83 Abs. 1:** transitional rule for complete applications filed by 04.02.2014 (Vertrauensschutz).

## Constitutional court, 09.05.2016

[Decision (PDF)](https://www.bayern.verfassungsgerichtshof.de/media/images/bayverfgh/14-vii-14-entscheidung-u.pdf)

| Case | Type | Brought by |

| Vf. 4-VIII-15 | Meinungsverschiedenheit, received 09.03.2015 | SPD group |

The respondents were the CSU group and the state government.

Result:

- **Constitutional:** 10H (Art. 82 Abs. 1–2), Art. 82 Abs. 3–4 and Art. 83 Abs. 1.
- **Void: only Art. 82 Abs. 5** (duty to seek agreement with neighbouring municipalities). The Land lacked the
  competence for it, which breached the Rechtsstaatsprinzip.

## Effect on wind expansion

From answers to written questions (details on [Bavarian questions](anfragen-bayern.md) and
[Bundestag questions](anfragen-bundestag.md)):

- **04.02.2014–30.09.2015:** only 10 new applications for wind turbines (BY Drs 17/9570).
- **21.11.2014–30.11.2015:** 73 turbines (200 MW) approved, 135 (337 MW) commissioned, mostly from applications filed
  before the cut-off date (BT-Drs 18/7274).
- **11/2014–03/2016:** 100 turbines (281.6 MW) approved. Municipalities planning below 10H: Oerlenbach, Altertheim,
  Sinzing, Walting, Langenzenn (BY Drs 17/12706).
